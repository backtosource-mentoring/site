Common questions

What we are asked most

Is every activity tailored to the organisation?

Yes. The fit call establishes the audience, the goal, the length and the organisational context, and the format is built accordingly.

How long is the talk?

The talk Who's Holding the Reins? usually runs 60–90 minutes. It can also be adapted to an event or another framework.

How long is the workshop?

The applied workshop usually runs about three hours. It can be extended into a series of sessions as needed.

Who is the boutique day for?

Usually a small group of roughly 3 to 13 managers and key roles who want a more personal space for conversation, practice and thinking.

How is the app used?

The app opens on your phone through a link. It gathers short tools for personal practice and opens from any "app" button on the site. Its interface is in Hebrew.

Can sessions run in English?

Yes. Talks and workshops can be delivered in English for international teams - mention it in your enquiry.

BOUTIQUE ACTIVITY

A space for learning,
practice, precision,
and integration.

Boutique activities are designed for managers, leadership teams, and professional groups who want to pause the pace of work, think more clearly, and return to action with tools they can apply.

The sessions take place in a carefully designed, high-standard space in Jaffa - bringing together professional content, hands-on practice, open conversation, and thoughtful hospitality.

02 / THE SPACE IS PART OF THE EXPERIENCE

A setting that makes room for different thinking - and for returning to work with greater precision.

Light, materials, openness, and carefully considered details create the conditions for focus, meaningful conversation, and learning that connects to reality.

03 / SHARED LEARNING

A lecture or workshop that connects to the work.

The shared room hosts lectures and workshops around a professional topic, a new language, and tools that can move into the work itself.

01Learn02Practice03Connect

04 / INTEGRATION

You do not need to remember everything.

A lecture or workshop may introduce three to five techniques. The goal is not to remember them all or apply everything at once.

One tool that continues into the work can be enough.

05 / FROM SHARED LEARNING TO FOCUSED WORK

Focused mentoring around a real case.

While the group continues working together, a manager can move into a separate room for focused one-to-one mentoring around a current case, challenge, or situation.

01Bring a case02Process the situation03Choose a step

Time to process the case, sharpen the question, and choose the next step.

06 / OUTCOME

Reach the finish line with energy for what comes next.

The goal is not to reach the finish line without effort. It is to reach it with greater clarity, a more precise way of working, and energy for the next task.

Leave the session with a clearer direction - and energy for what comes next.

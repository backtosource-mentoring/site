About

Esther Michal Katzir

Regulated, focused strength

I develop and apply a methodology for management and key roles that connects the body, regulation, clarity and decision-making. The purpose of my work is to create accessible, practical tools that help people interrupt the automatic response, return to attention and act from choice - even when the load is high.

The professional path

From 2016 to today

Since 2016 I have worked with individuals and groups, developing processes and tools that help people reach a better state of health, balance and functioning - physically and mentally.

Today, out of that experience, I bring the knowledge and the methodology to key roles and management as well - to the places where the ability to regulate yourself, stay clear and decide under load becomes a professional tool.

My work draws, among other things, on principles from the fields of neurobiology and regulation, but it is always translated into plain language and human practice. I believe knowledge becomes meaningful when it meets a real moment: a conversation that is hard to hold, a decision that has to be made, a team that needs leading, or a working day that will not slow down.

How I work

The starting point is the participants' reality

I do not arrive at an organisation with one template that fits everyone. In every talk, workshop or boutique day, the starting point is the participants' reality: what is happening for them, where the load is felt, and which conversation or capability they want to develop.

My formats combine knowledge, discussion, practice and a link to everyday situations. The aim is not to create more attractive slides, but to leave participants with a language, an attention and tools they can come back to.

Who I work with

People who lead people, decisions and processes

The work suits people who lead people, decisions and processes: managers, key roles, leadership teams and organisations that want to treat the quality of human functioning as part of how they work and lead.
